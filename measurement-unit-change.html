Case Study 01

From engineering issue to customer-facing documentation

Challenge

Developers identified that average transmission power values were being calculated incorrectly because the logarithmic nature of the dBm scale was not taken into account. The resulting change also affected how the related performance counters needed to be documented.

My contribution

I translated the engineering findings into concise customer-facing documentation explaining the change from dBm to mW and its impact on performance reporting.
