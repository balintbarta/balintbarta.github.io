Case Study 01

From engineering issue to customer-facing documentation

Translating a technical investigation involving RF power measurements into clear documentation focused on the system change and its practical impact for users.

Challenge

During a technical investigation, developers identified that average transmission power values were being calculated incorrectly because the logarithmic nature of the dBm scale was not taken into account. The resulting software change also affected the meaning and reporting of related performance counters.

The documentation therefore needed to explain the change accurately without exposing unnecessary implementation details or making the customer-facing description harder to understand.

My contribution

I assessed the documentation impact of the engineering findings and worked with the relevant technical stakeholders to understand the change. I then translated the technical information into concise, customer-facing documentation describing the change from dBm to mW and its effect on performance reporting.

I focused the final content on verified system behavior and user impact, presenting the change as a clearer and more useful way to interpret radio performance data.
